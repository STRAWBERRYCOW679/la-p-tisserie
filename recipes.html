Classic Vanilla Sponge Cake

Prep: 20m • Cook: 30m • Serves: 8
Ingredients
  • 200g plain flour
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 200g unsalted butter (softened)
  • 4 large eggs
  • 2 tsp baking powder, 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt, 50ml milk
Steps
  1. Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F). Grease and line two 20cm cake tins.
  2. Cream butter and sugar until pale. Beat in eggs one at a time with a spoonful of flour.
  3. Fold in remaining flour and baking powder, add vanilla and milk to loosen batter.
  4. Divide between tins, bake 25–30 minutes until golden and a skewer comes out clean.
  5. Cool on a wire rack, fill and frost as desired.
Tip: Room temperature ingredients give the lightest crumb.

Chocolate Fudge Brownie

Prep: 15m • Cook: 25m • Serves: 9
Ingredients
  • 200g dark chocolate, 175g butter
  • 325g caster sugar, 130g plain flour
  • 3 large eggs, 1 tsp vanilla
  • Pinch of salt, optional 100g chopped nuts
Steps
  1. Preheat oven to 170°C (340°F). Line a 20cm square tin.
  2. Melt chocolate and butter together; stir until smooth.
  3. Whisk in sugar, then eggs and vanilla. Fold in flour and salt (and nuts).
  4. Bake 20–25 minutes for a fudgy centre. Cool before slicing.
Tip: Slightly underbake for gooey brownies.

No-Bake Lemon Cheesecake

Prep: 25m • Chill: 4h • Serves: 8
Ingredients
  • 300g digestive biscuits, 100g butter (melted)
  • 500g cream cheese, 150g powdered sugar
  • 200ml double cream, zest & juice of 2 lemons
Steps
  1. Crush biscuits and mix with melted butter. Press into a springform tin.
  2. Whip cream until soft peaks. Beat cream cheese with sugar, fold in lemon juice and zest.
  3. Fold in whipped cream, pour onto base, chill 4 hours or overnight.
Tip: Use full‑fat cream for best texture.

Strawberry Banana Smoothie

Prep: 5m • Serves: 2
Ingredients
  • 2 ripe bananas, 250g strawberries
  • 250ml milk or yogurt, 1 tbsp honey (optional)
  • Ice as needed
Steps
  1. Blend all ingredients until smooth. Adjust sweetness to taste.
  2. Serve immediately with a strawberry garnish.
Tip: Freeze bananas for a thicker texture.
